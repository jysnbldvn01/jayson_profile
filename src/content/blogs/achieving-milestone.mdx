# Achieving Milestone: Adding a Blogs Section

Finally! The day has come. Between troubleshooting laptops at KMC, building out enrollment systems, and freelancing on the side, I somehow found time to add a blog section to my own portfolio. It's a small `git commit`, but a giant leap for my procrastination.

## The Journey

It started with a simple thought: "I should probably write about the stuff I build." That thought got buried under a school enrollment system, a barangay records platform, an AI audit tool, and approximately one thousand support tickets. Turns out building things for other people is easier than writing about my own.

### Why Now?

Somewhere between wiring up a PostgreSQL backend for MVAA and syncing calendars for instructors, I realized I had a lot of "why did I do it this way" moments worth documenting. Also, an empty blog tab on a portfolio site looks worse than no blog tab at all — and I couldn't let that slide.

## What to Expect?

- **Build Logs**: Notes from actually shipping things — enrollment systems, records platforms, and whatever freelance project lands on my desk next.
- **IT Survival Stories**: Lessons from a semester of hardware, software, and "have you tried turning it off and on again" troubleshooting.
- **Stack Opinions**: Why I keep reaching for React, Node, and Supabase, and when PHP still earns its spot.
- **Small Wins**: Because "it finally worked" deserves its own post sometimes.
